02 · What changed

The water is no longer assumed known.

The earlier five-parameter control asked whether concentrations alone could separate chemical inputs, one storage anomaly and decay — with discharge, K and X all known exactly. Your newer framing of the problem puts water and chemistry into a single inverse state. This is the smallest honest step in that direction.

Before · five parameters
  • Water discharge held fixed at the truth
  • Two chemical-input multipliers
  • Two decay rates
  • One chemical storage amplitude
  • One measured type: concentration
Now · eight parameters
  • Two water-input multipliers, west and east
  • Two chemical-input multipliers
  • Two decay rates, per day
  • Two storage amplitudes: one water, one chemical
  • Two measured types: discharge and concentration

The two regions are the existing metadata longitude classes split at the component median longitude (−96.306°, ties east), 32,289 and 32,290 reaches. They are coordinate classes, not hydrologic subbasins, and they are not connected. K, X and the topology stay fixed; there is no neural network anywhere in this experiment.

Why only eight

Each storage field is one fixed spatial pattern with a single fitted amplitude, not a free map. A general storage state has one degree of freedom per reach, and the per-reach source-versus-rate confounding recorded in the earlier work is untouched by this reduction.

The state is held in log coordinates for the six positive quantities (four multipliers, two decay rates) and in signed native units for the two storage amplitudes.

03 · Method

Coupled water and chemistry snapshot equations.

Cost

Separate weighted squared log residuals for Q and C, plus independent Gaussian prior penalties. The three costs are always reported separately.

Optimiser

SciPy L-BFGS-B on the box with JAX value_and_grad, float64, maxiter 500. No HMC, no 4D-Var, no time stepping.

Snapshot

One snapshot, no calendar. The nonzero storage anomalies mean it is not an equilibrium state. Chemical routing and decay are solved; what is absent is time resolution — no time-resolved pulse or transport run, and no temporal mass-balance test.

The objective, written out

J = ½ Σ_Q ((log Qpred − log yQ)/σQ)² + ½ Σ_C ((log Cpred − log yC)/σC)² + ½ Σ ((θ − prior mean)/prior sd)²

σQ = 0.0998 and σC = 0.1980 are assumed likelihood scales — √log(1+0.1²) and √log(1+0.2²), i.e. 10% and 20% CV. They are not the generated noise, which is exactly zero. Errors are assumed independent across gauges and between the two data types at the same gauge. That independence is an OSSE assumption: a real error covariance depends on the measurement protocol at each site.

Masked and held-out values are dropped from the closure entirely, so they can reach neither the value nor the gradient; the tests check that bitwise.

04 · OSSE design, frozen before any fit

Real river network, synthetic forcing and observations.

Domain and forcing
  • Outlet component of COMID 74072654: 64,579 reaches, one outlet, from the pinned region-74 archive, MD5 verified inside the run
  • Rref from a uniform 1 mm/day runoff depth; Rc,ref set to 1 mg/L of that water — a fixed reference field, not a link between the fitted multipliers
  • K, X and the topology come from the same archived static data and are held fixed, as are both storage-anomaly patterns; only the forcing and the observations are synthetic
Observations
  • 30 training reaches, drawn by the existing metadata-only mixed-area rule (reach ID, longitude class and drainage area only), 15 west and 15 east
  • 10 held-out reaches, reserved first — and reused from earlier experiments, so this is a paired evaluation, not a blind test
  • Both data types at the same reaches. Real chemistry stations are far sparser
  • Zero generated noise. Virtual observations equal the noise-free truth exactly
Prior

Independent Gaussians, sd (0.4, 0.4, 0.5, 0.5, 0.7, 0.7, 0.5, 0.5). No prior centre equals its truth: the truth sits 0.58–1.20 prior sd away. Box = prior mean ± 2 prior sd at every stage.

Controls

Three starting points frozen in advance — prior mean; 1.2 sd with the opposite sign to every truth offset; +1.5 sd everywhere. One prespecified weaker-prior control at prior sd ×3.

Gates

Recovered if |error| ≤ 0.25 base prior sd, marginal to 0.5. Train fit ≤ 0.10 and held-out ≤ 0.25 noise-scaled log RMSE. Optimiser convergence is not a recovery gate.

Storage patterns, and what they cost

Water storage: the existing tanh((lat − median)/2.5°) latitude dipole times the conservative equilibrium water storage. Chemical storage: tanh((lat − median)/2.5°)·tanh((lon − median)/5°), a lat–lon quadrupole, times the conservative equilibrium chemical mass. Choosing a chemical shape distinct from both the water dipole and the west/east classes favours separability; the realised cosine similarity between the two normalised storage flux patterns is 0.148, reported rather than engineered.

Both patterns are rescaled once so that four quantities stay positive over the whole tested box: Qw, Sw, Qc and Sc. That certificate costs identifiability, and the cost is quantified on the eighth slide.

05 · The registered prediction, confirmed

Concentration alone cannot see an overall scale. Discharge can.

The null direction

‖JCv‖/‖JC‖ = 9.6 × 10⁻¹⁷ along the predicted direction; the smallest right-singular vector aligns with it to twelve digits.

Discharge breaks it

The joint Jacobian's response along the same direction is 11.5, and its condition number is 24.9.

Read it narrowly

This is the local rank at the synthetic truth for this eight-parameter reduction — not a global, nor a reach-by-reach, identifiability result.

06 · Recovery against the prespecified tolerance

Seven of eight, and the same answer from every start.

The tolerance was chosen so that a fit which simply reverted to the prior mean would fail for every parameter: the smallest truth offset from a prior centre is 0.579 prior sd. Seven parameters clear it by a factor of three or more.

07 · Ten locations held out of the objective

Predictions at ten held-out measurement locations.

Held out, noise-scaled log RMSE

Discharge 0.038, concentration 0.109 — both inside the prespecified 0.25.

Against no fitting

The prior-mean baseline gives training log RMSE 0.250 (Q) and 0.364 (C), against 0.0035 and 0.023 after fitting.

Not a blind test

These ten locations were scored in earlier experiments on the same network. The topology is known and every reach is routed; what is held out is the observation. They support a paired comparison, and nothing stronger.

08 · The one that did not come back

The chemical storage amplitude is held by the prior.

Not an optimiser failure

Every fit reported success, |g|∞ ≤ 9.8 × 10⁻⁷, no active bounds, three starts agreeing to 2 × 10⁻⁸ prior sd. A positive-definite regularised Hessian is not proof of identification.

Weaker than the prior

Marginal data information 0.66 against a prior of 1.0; posterior variance reduction 0.40, against ≥ 0.91 for the other seven. The weakest joint mode is 98.5% this parameter.

Predicted, not discovered

Scalar linear-Gaussian shrinkage predicts −0.72 prior sd at the base prior and −0.17 at ×3; observed −0.678 and −0.168.

The cause is structural rather than numerical: requiring guaranteed positive flux and positive storage over a ±2-prior-sd box caps how much storage signal can exist at all. At truth the chemical storage share of total flux has median 3.1% at the 30 training gauges, against an assumed error scale of 19.8% — about one sixth of its assumed noise per gauge. Water storage, at 6.0% against 9.98%, is recovered.

09 · What was checked, and what did not pass

234 tests, one prespecified criterion missed.

Verified
  • 234 tests pass — 212 pre-existing plus 22 new for this experiment
  • Independent dense-matrix oracle at three parameter vectors, all eight output fields to rtol 1e-11
  • Finite-difference gradients in all eight directions (rtol 3e-6) and the full prior-scaled Jacobian in both data blocks (rtol 2e-5)
  • Held-out and masked values cannot move the value or the gradient — checked bitwise
  • Positivity certificate checked against all 256 box corners; admissible at truth and at every fit
Failed, or not claimed
  • Concentration training fit 0.116 against a prespecified 0.10 noise-scaled log RMSE — a genuine miss, caused by the prior holding ΔSc away from truth
  • Fixed storage patterns were chosen to favour separation of the two amplitudes
  • No time evolution, no temporal conservation test, no real chemistry validation
  • No calibrated posterior: the Gauss-Newton covariance is a local linearisation, not a sample
  • No complete MATLAB parity check for this configuration

Signed storage contributions are preserved and never clipped: at truth 32,286 of 64,579 water storage-flux contributions and 43,825 chemical ones are negative. Positive totals inside the bounded tested domain are an algebraic property of that box — they do not validate the underlying storage physics.

Where the miss comes from

Held-out errors are essentially equal to training errors for both data types (0.038 vs 0.036 for discharge, 0.109 vs 0.116 for concentration), so there is no sign of overfitting. The residual is coherent, not noise: the prior pulls the chemical storage amplitude to 0.26 instead of 0.60, which leaves a structured concentration residual worth about 0.12 of the assumed error. Under the weaker-prior control the same training figure falls to 0.026.

10 · Discussion

Two things to confirm, then one diagnostic.

Question 1 · what ΔS means

We read ΔS as an anomaly relative to the equilibrium storage of the current inputs, following your archived April equation draft — not S[t+1] − S[t]. Every number on this deck depends on that reading.

Question 2 · the chemical storage law

For the admissibility bound we used Sc,eq = (5/3) K Qc,eq as our provisional analogue of the water residence time. The draft gives τ = 5K/3 for water and states no chemical equilibrium-storage identity. The placement of Φ on the chemical storage term is yours, kept exactly as drafted.

The diagnostic we would run next — not yet run

Find where the chemical storage signal actually is, then test whether observing there would help: locate storage-sensitive locations, then re-run the joint information and recovery checks with the other seven parameters still free, because a single sensitivity column says nothing about marginal information once nuisance parameters move. At truth the chemical storage share of flux reaches 18.0% somewhere in the network against a median of 3.1% at the 30 metadata-chosen gauges.

Read it as an oracle feasibility diagnostic: it uses the truth point, so it can indicate whether the information could exist. It would not settle “gauge design versus structural limit”, and it is not a deployable monitoring design.

How we would run it

Step one is cheap: one forward-mode Jacobian column of |∂ log C / ∂ bc| · sd / σC over all 64,579 reaches at the truth point, seconds of compute, to rank locations by chemical-storage sensitivity.

Step two is the one that carries the weight, and the one a ranking alone cannot replace: for a candidate set of locations, rebuild the full eight-parameter prior-scaled Jacobian, then report the marginal data information for ΔSc with every other parameter free, together with a repeat of the recovery test. A column norm can look large while the marginal information stays below the prior if the new rows also load on the chemical input and decay directions.

Even a favourable result would be an upper bound obtained at the synthetic truth, on one noise-free realization, under the same positivity certificate and the same single fixed storage pattern.

Appendix C · diagnostics

Where the information actually is.

Joint spectrum at truth

Data-information eigenvalues 378, 289, 82, 79, 17, 13, 12, 0.61. The weakest mode is 98.5% ΔSc.

Correlations

Largest posterior correlation 0.61, chemical input east with decay east. Nothing exceeds 0.9: source-versus-rate confounding is visible but not binding at this reduction.

Read as a linearisation

The covariance adds the prior identity, so it is positive definite even where the data carry nothing. It is not a sampled posterior.
